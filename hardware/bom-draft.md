## Rack-space / fit check before ordering

The T2 provides 12U. A preliminary allocation is already tight: touchscreen (2U for DP-0059; 1U for the compact alternative), patch panel and cable management (about 1.5U together), three mini-PC trays (about 3U if mounted separately), GPU node shelf (about 1–2U), NAS shelf (about 1–2U), router/switch shelf (about 1U), cooling panel (2U), storage drawer (2U), and UPS (often another 1–2U). With the 2U display, this rough total is approximately 14–17U before accounting for device height, front/rear connector clearance, and airflow; the 1U display alternative saves one U.

Treat the T2 as the design reference, but do not order all hardware assuming it will fit in one T2. The GPU PC, NAS, and battery backup are the key unknowns. Measure or obtain their case dimensions and power draw, then make a scaled rack layout. The final design may need fewer accessories, custom mounts, a second/large enclosure, or the UPS mounted outside the T2.

## External cable plan

Only these three cables should leave the finished enclosure:

1. AC power into the UPS / power section
2. ISP 1 WAN handoff to the dual-WAN router
3. ISP 2 WAN handoff to the dual-WAN router

All router-to-switch and switch-to-device Ethernet connections stay inside the enclosure and are patched neatly through the internal patch panel.
